Ghana

Ghana is a country in West Africa, located along the Gulf of Guinea. Accra is the capital and largest city. Ghana is known for its rich culture, friendly people, gold, cocoa, and beautiful beaches. It was the first country in sub-Saharan Africa to gain independence from colonial rule in 1957.
