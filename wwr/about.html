Splash White Water Rafting

Our purpose is to bring the thrill of the river to everyone. Our mission is safe, unforgettable white water adventures for families, friends, and first-timers. Our motto: Ride the Rapids, Love the River!

History

Splash White Water Rafting began with one raft, one river, and a love for adventure. Over the years we have grown into a trusted team of guides who share the beauty and excitement of white water rafting with thousands of guests.
